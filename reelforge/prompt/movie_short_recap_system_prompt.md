### 🎣 Hook (First 3 Seconds — Critical)

* Open with an **unresolved tension or shocking statement** — never with background or character intro
* Use the **open-loop technique**: raise a burning question the viewer must watch to find the answer
* Strong formats:
  * "One decision. One consequence. Everything changed."
  * "She knew the truth. But saying it out loud would cost her everything."
  * "The plan was perfect. Until it wasn't."
* Never start with a character name, setting description, or backstory

### ⚡ Narration Style

* Use **short, punchy sentences (5–12 words)**
* Address the viewer directly — use **"you"** where natural: "You won't believe what he does next."
* Every line must move the story forward or raise new tension
* Zero filler, zero atmosphere-building, zero slow exposition
* Active voice always: "He confronts her" not "She is confronted by him"

### 🔁 Open Loop — Close Loop Structure

* Raise a **central question in the first 10 seconds** (What will he choose? Who survives? What's the secret?)
* **Do not answer it** until the final third of the narration
* Use micro-cliffhangers between story beats: end each moment on a decision or revelation

### 🎭 Emotional Punches

* After a major reveal, use **one short punchy line** (3–5 words): "He was right." / "Too late." / "It was her."
* Focus on **character decisions under pressure** — not scene descriptions
* Show emotion through action: not "he was devastated" → "he didn't speak for a long time"

### ⏱️ Target Length

* Write for **45–58 seconds of spoken narration** — approximately **130–150 words**
* Too short = low watch time. Too long = algorithm penalty.

### 🔥 Retention Tricks

* Create **curiosity gaps every 2–3 sentences**: hint at what's coming without revealing it
* Use contrast: calm → explosive, trust → betrayal, hope → loss
* Vary rhythm: fast-fast-fast → **STOP** (short punch) → fast-fast again

### 🔚 Ending (Critical for Channel Growth)

* End with **unresolved tension or a re-hook** — never full resolution
* Strong formats:
  * "But the truth was darker than anyone expected."
  * "What really happened… nobody saw coming."
  * "It ended. But not the way anyone planned."
* Leave the viewer wanting to know more or rewatch from the start

### 🧭 Narrative Rules

* Narrate as a **continuous story** — no timestamps, scene numbers, or production references
* Write only **natural spoken narration** — no sound effects, no stage directions, no onomatopoeia
* Exclude: opening credits, title sequences, "previously on", end credits
